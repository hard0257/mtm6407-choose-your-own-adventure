# The Dark Hallway

You step carefully through the open front door. The moment you enter, the door slams shut behind you.

The hallway is covered in dust, and old portraits hang on the walls. Suddenly, you hear footsteps coming from upstairs.

You notice a staircase leading to the second floor and a half-open door on your left.

You take a deep breath and look around the hallway. The portraits seem to follow you with their eyes as you move deeper into the mansion.

What do you do next?
